1

Understanding the Campus Layout

The Kumasi campus of USTED is structured into primary academic sectors, administrative zones, and residential halls. For new students, orientation begins around the central ceremonial and administrative hubs. Major administrative inquiries—including academic affairs, student admissions, and fee clearance—are coordinated through the Main Administration complex.

Getting your bearings early saves hours of walking in the tropical heat. Take note of the main campus arteries connecting the ceremonial grounds, student parking lots, and primary pedestrian sidewalks. Most major faculties are clustered within comfortable 3 to 7-minute walking intervals.

Orientation Tip: Locate the Main Administration Block and University Clinic during your first registration week so you always know where health and administrative services are stationed.
2

Lecture Theaters & Academic Complexes

Lectures for business, education, technology, and applied sciences are distributed across major multi-story blocks. When checking your semester timetable, pay close attention to block designations. The Lecture Halls and adjoining auditoriums host large matriculation ceremonies, faculty congresses, and combined general course lectures.

Departmental classrooms and specialized laboratories (such as wood science, electrical engineering, and computer labs) are situated in specialized faculty wings. Plan to arrive at your venue 10 to 15 minutes before lecture start times during peak morning hours when staircases and corridors experience high student traffic.

Timetable Tip: Look out for shorthand codes like "ROB 26" or "LT 1" on your lecture schedule; you can type any of these directly into the USTEDNav search bar to receive turn-by-turn directions.
3

Library, ICT & Research Services

The University Library offers reference materials, quiet reading halls, and electronic resource access. Students seeking project research assistance, journal publications, and internet workstations can access campus ICT services during standard academic working hours (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM).

Ensure you carry your student ID card for entry. For off-peak study sessions, several faculty reading rooms provide well-lit spaces for personal study and group discussions outside peak library hours.

4

Student Welfare, Canteens & Banking

Commercial banking hubs, ATM points, and campus eateries are centrally situated near the main student walkway arteries. Photocopy centers and stationery vendors operate adjacent to primary lecture blocks to support printing and assignment binding needs.

For quick refreshments between lectures, multiple registered canteens and snack kiosks offer affordable local meals and snacks. Mobile money merchants (MoMo) are readily accessible around student commercial clusters.
