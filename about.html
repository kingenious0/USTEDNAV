Project Mission

USTEDNav is an interactive digital campus directory and navigation platform designed specifically for the Akenten Appiah-Menka University of Skills Training and Entrepreneurial Development (AAMUSTED) Kumasi campus. Built to assist freshers, continuing students, faculty, and campus visitors, USTEDNav provides seamless spatial guidance across academic complexes, administrative blocks, lecture theaters, and student welfare facilities.

Whether you need to find a specific lecturer's office in ROB Block, the exact examination room inside the New Great Hall, or the nearest banking and health facility, USTEDNav removes the guesswork with turnkey walking directions and verified spatial footprints.

Commitment to Open Accessibility

Navigating an expanding tertiary campus can be challenging for new students. USTEDNav bridges this gap by delivering lightweight, mobile-first progressive web tools that function smoothly even under fluctuating mobile network connectivity.

Our data is curated through active on-the-ground mapping and feedback from the university community. Vector campus basemaps, routing graphs, and directory search indexes are cached locally on-device so you never lose your way during lectures or matriculation ceremonies.
